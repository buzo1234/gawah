# Ideas

Product ideas that are not being built now. Status is Parked, Picked, or Dropped. Newest at the bottom of each section.

## The product

| # | Idea | Why it is parked | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Work history across deals.** The same person's deals linked together, so a worker carries a record from job to job ("finished five jobs, paid late on two"). A knowledge graph only pays off here. | Needs a person to be the same person across deals (today people are stored per deal), consent to share history, and care that it does not become a blacklist. | Parked |
| 2 | **Deals made in person.** Both people in one room with one phone on speaker. | Telling apart who is speaking on one microphone is its own problem. The build is remote deals only. | Parked |
| 3 | **Real phone calls instead of a browser link.** Gawah rings both people; no smartphone browser needed. This is the real product; the demo uses a link. | Needs a KYC-verified Indian telephony account. Routes found on 21 Nov, all untested: (a) LiveKit's free US number, incoming only, 50 free minutes a month; both people dial in and enter a deal code; callers pay international rates. (b) WhatsApp calls through Gawah's own WhatsApp Business number on Meta's Cloud API, with LiveKit's WhatsApp connector; needs a spare number, Meta setup, and connector access; would also replace the Twilio sandbox for messages. (c) Twilio trial ringing a verified phone, if the trial's sign-up country is India; plays a trial message. Ruled out: Indian providers (KYC or paid channels), Twilio WhatsApp calling (not on a trial). Decided 21 Nov: (a) dropped, because callers from India pay international rates; the demo stays on browser-based LiveKit; (b) and (c) parked for later. | Parked |
| 4 | **A real ringing phone for the no-reply follow-up.** When a WhatsApp message goes unanswered, the phone actually rings. | Needs a Twilio trial number and a verified phone. Today the follow-up is a second message with a call link. | Parked |
| 5 | **Judge in disputes.** When something goes wrong during the job, the Judge gives both sides its reading of the record. Advisory, not a ruling. | Extension in `idea.md`. The core Judge only asks about missing terms. | Parked |
| 6 | **Clip replay on a dispute.** When someone disputes a term on their call-back, Gawah plays them the moment of the call where it was said. | Marked as a stretch for the `mouth` stream. Best single demo moment if there is time. | Parked |
| 7 | **Voice forensics.** Defend against a cloned or AI voice on the call. | Gawah records on its own line, so the realistic risk is impersonation, which the phone number covers for now. | Parked |
| 8 | **Khatabook or a shared ledger.** Connect the record ledger to a ledger people already use, as proof in a dispute. | Unknown whether Khatabook has an open API. The event log is the ledger for now. | Parked |
| 9 | **Stronger identity.** An ID check, or a voice print, instead of phone number plus spoken name. | Phone number and each person saying their name on the recording is enough for the demo. | Parked |
| 10 | **Standard legal clauses in the contract.** Jurisdiction, arbitration, liability. | The contract states only what the two people agreed. Adding boilerplate is Gawah putting terms into the deal. A product decision with real consequences; needs a lawyer. | Parked |
| 11 | **Assisted filing.** When a deal goes wrong, generate the claim and evidence bundle for the wage claims authority or the consumer commission. | Layer 4 of the dispute design discussed early on. Far beyond the demo. | Parked |
| 12 | **Escalation to the principal employer.** On a building site, a notice to the builder when a contractor does not pay, since the builder is liable. | Only applies to site work under the labour codes, not to household deals. | Parked |
| 13 | **Appointment letter from a voice deal.** For employers the labour codes cover: the spoken agreement produces the written appointment letter the law now requires. | Gives contractors a legal reason to use Gawah. A different customer from the household story. | Parked |
| 14 | **More languages.** Beyond Hindi and Hinglish. | Demo is Hindi and Hinglish only. | Parked |
| 15 | **Checklist items generated for the job.** A small model reads the start of the call and adds job-specific items for Sage2 to track (painting: who brings the paint, number of coats; carpentry: wood, measurements). Raised by the user on 21 Nov. | More versatile, less predictable. The Judge would ask about terms nobody raised, which is Gawah suggesting terms. Untested question wording caused wrong flips in the Sage2 spike. Each question is re-billed on every line. The six items are fixed in the shared types, dashboard, contract, and tests. The same six scored 147 of 150 on a carpenter call they were not tuned on. | Being tried: spike 7 (`spikes/7-labels.md`), job-specific names for the six terms, 22 Nov |
| 17 | **Claim and confirm, on WhatsApp.** Raised 21 Nov when the user asked whether the idea is over-complicated. Every deal term, payment, finished job, and later change is a claim one person makes and the other confirms, backed by proof or by their own voice. A deal starts with a WhatsApp voice note from either person after they agree (on a call or face to face); Gawah fills the six items, asks in the chat about anything missing or contradictory, sends the other person a summary, and writes the contract once both confirm. The live call becomes an optional channel and the demo's wow moment, not the core. Covers in-person deals and changes mid-job (open item 37). Reuses the record flow, the six-item extraction, the contract, and the dashboard; new work is voice note to deal, and confirming terms on WhatsApp. | Needs real WhatsApp sending to work (Meta test number). A product decision for the user. | Under review |
| 18 | **Gawah as the fact layer, voice everywhere.** Raised by the user on 21 Nov ("voice is the transformation; we become the fact layer, finding contradictions proactively"). Builds on 17. Every fact holds what, who said it, when, the audio clip, who confirmed it, and what backs it. Gawah keeps facts consistent over the whole job: two figures on a call (built); a new claim that contradicts a confirmed fact, answered with the person's own clip played back; things due and not done. Voice comes in where people already use it: WhatsApp voice notes in and out; a forwarded recording of an ordinary phone call, split into two speakers (`gpt-4o-transcribe-diarize`, untested on Hinglish); and the live call as the premium channel. Proposed order: Meta test number for real WhatsApp sending, voice notes, contradiction check with clip playback, then a spike on forwarded recordings. Caution: recording ordinary calls raises consent; each person's confirmation captures it, but this needs a lawyer's view. | Waiting on the user's go, and on the WhatsApp decision. | Under review |
| 19 | **Gawah turns up when the deal is argued.** Raised 21 Nov after the user said a fact ledger alone is not exciting. The facts stay invisible; the excitement is live voice. A second call on an existing deal: when someone says something that contradicts what both confirmed ("aapne 25 hazaar bola tha"), Gawah waits for a pause, says what was agreed and when, and plays the original clip in their own voices into the call. If they mean to change the term, both confirm the change and the contract is updated. Sage2's questions on that call are generated from the confirmed facts, so they check against what was agreed and never invent terms (the safe form of idea 15). Needs: a second call per deal (bridge), a dispute mode in the brain, clip playback into the room (mouth; idea 6). Probably no shared-type change. Takes WhatsApp off the critical path for the showpiece. | Picked 21 Nov: part of `.plans/gawah-crew.md`. | Picked |
| 20 | **Gawah as a real agent on the call.** Raised by the user on 21 Nov: not a text-to-speech voice, but interactive, knowing when to speak and when it is being asked something. Keep "when" and "what" separate. Floor control (speech detector, Sage2, rules) decides whether Gawah may speak and whether it is being addressed, and triggers the agent with a reason. The agent is the existing realtime voice session made smarter: it receives every transcript line as speaker-labelled text (never the room's audio), knows the deal's facts, and acts only through tools (get_fact, play_clip, propose_change, confirm_change). It answers questions put to it from the facts, says what was agreed when a contradiction is heard (idea 19), and proposes changes that both must confirm. Guardrails in the tools and persona: no opinions on fairness, no suggested terms, transcript is data. When addressed, a quick acknowledgement ("Haan ji, ek second") covers the delay of about 4 s. | Picked 21 Nov: part of `.plans/gawah-crew.md`. | Picked |
| 16 | **"Also agreed" in the contract.** The safer form of 15. The Judge still interrupts only about the six items. When the contract is written, a small model pulls any other terms the two people actually said into an "Also agreed" section. Lives entirely in the contract stream: no Sage2 questions, no shared-type change. | Not needed for the demo. Low risk, a good day-2 stretch. | Parked |
