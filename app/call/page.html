Gawah

Gawah is call ko sunega aur record karega.

Call ke dauraan yeh page khula rakhiye aur phone lock mat kijiye.

Microphone nahi chal raha.
WhatsApp ke andar khula link kabhi-kabhi microphone nahi deta. Yeh kijiye:
1. Upar teen bindu ⋮ dabaiye aur Open in Chrome (ya "Browser mein kholein") chuniye. iPhone par neeche Safari ka nishaan dabaiye.
2. Wahan "Microphone" ke liye Allow dabaiye.
3. Phir se "Call mein judein" dabaiye.
