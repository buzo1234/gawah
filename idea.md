## Back Story

Indian Labour laws took effect on 21st November 2025 - Exactly one year from today.

It brought in four new Labour codes covering wages, industrial relations, social security and occupational safety, health and working conditions.

### Did it impact?

It had a mixed impact.
It expanded long term social security and formal protections.
It reduced immediate take-home pay and loosened job security rules.

Lower take-home pay actually means higher savings - more into PF.

Bigger gratuity payouts - pro-rata after just 1 year instead of earlier 5 years.

Faster settlements - to be done by employer within 2 working days of an employee's resignation, dismissal, or retrenchment.

## Where's the pain today?

Problem is on both sides - workers go unpaid, and people paying them get cheated too.

## The Story

Project name: Gawah (Witness)

Eg:

Today, Sunita wants to hire Ramesh but neither trusts each other, and each fears the same thing from the opposite side.

Our tagline:
`India's economy runs on zubaan. We give that word a witness.`

### Evidence of the Problem

- Most workers are informal: Estimates put 80% to 90% of India's workforce in informal employment. [SBI Research](https://sbi.bank.in/documents/13958/14472/08052026_PLFS_SBI+RESEARCH.pdf/e97cfeb9-95fb-cf06-19f3-029ac971fd5e?t=1778220646800)

- Even salaried jobs are mostly unwritten: About 58% of regular wage and salaried employees have no written job contract.

- In construction, written contracts barely exist: A tier 1 city survey found 0.4% of workers had one, and an ILO (International Labour organisation) study found 1.7%.

- Impact to economy: Informal sector is roughly 45% of GDP.

### Why voice?

- India really does run on Zubaan.
- Laws like Indian Labour laws still demand paper that many workers can't read, and we can bridge the two.

## The Idea

A connected system across calling and messaging that joins group calls between a hirer and a worker (how and when do they join the call is something we need to answer).
It acts both as a witness and a legal contract maker making sure that contract is going correctly as a neutral judge.

After the call - it calls both the parties independently to explain the terms. Post which - it drafts a legal contract and sends to both the parties.

I think we should add system of records in core idea rather than extension right now as it seems important to have. If we can use already existing systems - then the friction of using our product can be decreased. So that we are not just solving half part of the story.

## Extentions

Proof of records: Terms, attendance and payments captured.

Forensic checks - to defend against AI voice.
