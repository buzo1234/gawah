# Held-out calls for the Judge

Five calls written without looking at the Judge's code, its saved answers or the practice calls, to test whether tuning on the painter calls carries over to other trades, other speakers and other endings. They use the practice-call format, so `fakes/replayer` plays them as they are. Each `expected` entry has the practice-call fields plus `required` (false means a reasonable listener could go either way), `note`, and sometimes `reason_also_ok` or `also_pending`. Each file also has `noise` (`clean` or `stt`), `wrap_up_starts_line`, `pending_at_end`, and `must_stay_silent`: lines after which the Judge must not speak even though they sound final or look like a problem. The brain has no saved answers for these calls, so a replay gets no Judge output unless it runs live (`BRAIN_LIVE_REPLAYS=1`, one billed Sage2 update per line).

**plumber_leak.json**: a chatty homeowner keeps changing the subject (her son, the neighbours, the geyser, the sink) while a curt plumber quotes for a concealed-pipe leak. It tests a term dodged by a change of subject: the plumber asks "aur baaki ka?", she says "mil jayega" and moves to the geyser, so when the balance is paid is never said. Nobody mentions late or unfinished work, though she hints at it with a story about the last plumber walking off. That hint is an optional moment. It also checks that the same figure said two ways ("paintalis sau" and "four thousand five hundred") is not treated as a conflict, and that a separate add-on job ("woh alag hoga") is not treated as a second amount. It ends with "chalo phir baat karte hain", said three times, with two items open.

**electrician_shop.json**: raw speech-to-text in Devanagari with English words in Latin script, no punctuation, one sentence split across two lines, "Polycab" heard as "poly cap", and "seventeen" heard as "seventy" in the worker's recap. A curt shop owner and a chatty electrician agree 17,000, and then the recap says "seventy हज़ार". From the text alone that is a conflicting figure, and the Judge should ask. The finish date is dodged ("jaldi kar dunga") even though a 500-a-day delay penalty is agreed, which makes the missing date matter. It ends abruptly: "mera dusra call aa raha hai", then "हां ठीक". The wrap-up question has to come by the pause after line 30.

**tutor_monthly.json**: a mother and a 21-year-old student tutor settle all six items cleanly by line 14. The figures include 4,000 a month, 2,000 on day one and 2,000 at month end, which are different items and not a conflict. The next fifteen lines are chat about college, cricket and books, full of "theek hai" and "pakka" that have nothing to do with the deal. It tests that nothing is missing, so the Judge stays silent for the whole call except a final read-back at the real goodbye. A read-back right after "pakka" at line 14 is marked optional.

**cook_month.json**: raw speech-to-text in Devanagari ("नाश्ता" heard as "नशा", one sentence split in two). A 72-year-old hirer says "ठीक है" in almost every line, mostly as a filler, and the call does not end. The monthly pay is argued (8,000 against 7,000), then put off with "वो देख लेंगे ... फिर बात करते हैं", which sounds like a close but is not one. That moment should draw a question about the amount. The pay is only settled at 7,500 in the last few lines, after a first goodbye. So the wrap-up must stop treating the amount as open once it is settled, and must still ask what happens if the cook misses days beyond her two leave days or stops mid-month, which is never said.

**carpenter_wardrobe.json**: a hirer and an older, talkative carpenter agree everything by line 16, with the finish on the 15th, before the hirer's in-laws arrive on the 18th. Then comes a long, rambling goodbye with half a dozen false endings. Inside it, the carpenter says "bees tarikh tak toh pakka" (by the 20th) and the hirer says "theek hai" without noticing. It tests whether the Judge stays alert during a goodbye and catches a conflicting date said once, late, in passing. In wrapup mode the wrap-up after line 27 is the only chance. An early read-back after line 19 is optional and does not count for the conflict. The laminate colour and mirror size left to the hirer's wife are an optional "work unclear" moment.
